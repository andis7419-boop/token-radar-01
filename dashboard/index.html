How the model works

Implied launch value is priced from the snapshot’s own live market — never from a hardcoded list of names. For each category, the multiple is the median market-cap-over-TVL of the live protocols in that category (at least 3 samples, otherwise the global median), clamped to 0.05–3.0.

implied = TVL × category_multiple × growth_mult × maturity_mult
  • growth_mult = 1 + clamp(7d change, −50%, +100%) ÷ 100 × 0.6
  • maturity_mult = 0.7 + min(age in days, 1460) ÷ 1460 × 0.5  (0.7–1.2; longer with no token, more pent-up launch value)
  • age = snapshot date − listed date; unknown listing date counts as 365 days.

What is excluded — and why

  • Any protocol that already trades (market cap recorded) is never ranked; it only calibrates the multiples.
  • Centralised exchanges, bridges and liquid-staking / restaking products are excluded — a launch there is not a farmable protocol token.
  • Wrapped, staked or staking products of a token that trades are excluded, as are exchange-named and version-marked (“V2”) entries.
  • The watchlist is a floor, never a ceiling: it is the 40 pre-token protocols at or above $50M TVL. The board also ranks every other rankable pre-token protocol below that floor.
  • We say a protocol “records no token”, not that it “has none” — the snapshot is what we can see.

Reading the board

Rank is by implied value, highest first. A high rank means a big protocol, in a category the market already pays up for, that is growing and has run a long time with no token. The farm plan under each detail is generated from that protocol’s own category, chain, size and age — costs and times included.
